# Venue terms: one table

Every venue whose data this project reads or considered reading, what its terms say about the four things that matter
here, and the decision taken. The quotes and dates come from the dated readings in `docs/venues.md` (API facts, fee
entries and the per-venue reading notes) and the terms table in `docs/research/2026-10-pre-ipo-divergence.md`; this file
collects them in one place and adds nothing. A reading, not legal advice. Re-read before any commercial use, and re-check
a venue's page before relying on a clause: terms change.

Posture (Sam, 2026-10-27 and 2026-10-28): non-commercial personal research. The page shows figures derived from public
read-only APIs (net edges, capacities, bases, episodes), never raw quotes or order books (`DASHBOARD_SHOW_BOOKS = False`),
and nothing derived from Schwab data leaves the OptiPlex. The CF Benchmarks (BRTI) and Binance series that feed the
ladder basis bound are never republished; only the bound is. Written authorization from a venue is the route to anything
more.
